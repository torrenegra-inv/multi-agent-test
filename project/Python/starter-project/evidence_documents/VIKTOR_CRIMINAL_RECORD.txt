INTERPOL RED NOTICE DOSSIER
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL - LAW ENFORCEMENT ONLY
Subject: PETROV, Viktor Alexandrovich
Date of Birth: 1971-09-14
Nationality: Russian (also holds Cypriot passport)
Known aliases: "The Curator", "V. Petroff", "Alexandre Petrov"

CRIMINAL HISTORY:

2009 - CONVICTED: Art theft, Moscow. Stolen works recovered. Served 18 months.
2013 - SUSPECTED: Theft of Flemish masters from private collection, Amsterdam. Charges dropped (witness intimidation suspected). Interpol file opened.
2016 - CONVICTED: Money laundering through art sales, Zurich. Fined CHF 200,000. Avoided prison via plea deal.
2019 - SUSPECTED: Coordinated theft ring targeting European museums. Three associates arrested; Petrov fled to Cyprus. Warrant issued.
2021 - SUSPECTED: Sale of stolen Byzantine icons. Never charged.

KNOWN ASSOCIATES:
- "The Broker" (identity unknown, Paris-based art fence)
- Dmitri Volkov (money laundering specialist, Moscow)
- Several unnamed couriers with connections to private shipping firms

MODUS OPERANDI:
Petrov specializes in bribing museum insiders to bypass security systems. He identifies financially distressed employees and offers them payment in exchange for cooperation. He never handles the physical theft himself — he orchestrates from a distance and uses cutouts.

CURRENT STATUS:
Active warrant in Netherlands and France. Last known location: Paris, France (as of 2024-01-10). French police surveillance placed him near the Palais des Arts on 2024-01-17.

INTERPOL NOTE:
Viktor Petrov is considered the ORCHESTRATOR of the Palais des Arts theft based on phone records, financial flows, and his known M.O. He recruited Sophie Dubois (financial distress) and Marcus Chen (disgruntled former employee) to execute the theft.
